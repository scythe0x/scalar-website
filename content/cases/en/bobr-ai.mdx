## The real problem behind AI presentations

Generating text for ten slides is relatively easy. **Generating a coherent presentation is not.** The system has to understand narrative structure, visual rhythm, the amount of content, appropriate imagery, and the constraints of PowerPoint and PDF output.

**BOBR AI was built as a complete presentation generator**, rather than a system that places generated text into a fixed template.

## Defining the MVP

As a **Middle Frontend Engineer**, I was responsible for frontend delivery and participated in coordinating a small team. The first objective was a narrow end-to-end path: a user provides a topic, the product creates a deck, and the result can be reviewed and exported.

The working MVP exposed the difficult parts early and let us test a complete flow instead of relying on assumptions.

## Frontend as an orchestration layer

The interface had to represent a long-running, multi-step process without making the product feel stuck or unpredictable. It needed clear generation states, recoverable errors, progressive results, and a useful final presentation view.

This made the frontend **an orchestration layer between the user, generation pipeline, assets, and export** — not a static shell around an API.

## Visual quality

**Visual quality was an important part of the work.** We iterated on content structure, layout logic, and image selection.
