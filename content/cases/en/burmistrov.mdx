## A compact scope

The task was deliberately compact: **create a useful personal website** for business consultant and executive coach Viktor Burmistrov without turning it into a long branding project.

The site needed to **explain who he is immediately**, surface useful materials and recent social content, and make the next action obvious.

## Scope before decoration

We agreed on the **information structure** quickly:

1. identity and professional role;
2. primary consultation and course actions;
3. useful materials and recent Reels;
4. concise background and approach;
5. social destinations.
