## Product context

Begemot AI turns a topic into a structured educational document through a multi-stage generation flow. A user previews the result, purchases the full work, and can add illustrations or a presentation before export.

The difficult frontend problem was not the initial generation screen. It was everything that happened after generation: reviewing a long document, understanding its structure, editing it safely, and connecting those actions to the purchase funnel.

## A generated document still needs a real editor

Generated text is not a finished artifact. Users need to navigate sections, correct individual blocks, keep the document structurally valid, and understand what will appear in the exported version.

I built a full editing layer around a customized Editor.js implementation. The editor had to work with generated content rather than a blank document, handle uncommon states, and coexist with legacy product flows.

## Delivering the redesign

I implemented a complete interface redesign from Figma and brought it to production. This involved more than rebuilding visual components: new screens had to preserve existing business behavior while the codebase still contained older implementation decisions.

The work included:

- translating designs into reusable frontend patterns;
- aligning new flows with existing product logic;
- fixing critical issues across new and legacy code;
- handling rare states that appeared in long-running generation flows;
- keeping releases stable while the interface changed.

## Making the funnel observable

Product discussions become more useful when the team can see what users actually do. I implemented client-side analytics and worked with Metabase funnels to inspect the path from generation and preview to purchase and additional options.

That changed the frontend role from simply delivering screens to helping the team reason about the product.

## Working at product scale

For a significant period I was the only frontend engineer on Begemot AI. A second frontend developer joined later. My responsibility was therefore not limited to one feature area: I had to keep new work, legacy stability, product analytics, and delivery moving together.

## Result

Begemot AI received a production redesign, a real editing experience for generated documents, stronger frontend stability, and an observable product funnel—all while serving a product with more than one million users.
